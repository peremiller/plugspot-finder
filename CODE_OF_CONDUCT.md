# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PlugSpot welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, protect private information, and focus discussion on improving the product and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe and constructive community.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
